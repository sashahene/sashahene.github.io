I designed an AI-assisted research library that catalogs peer-reviewed studies on transdermal alcohol sensing and wearable monitoring, helping the team turn research into prototype requirements.

Initial Prototype

Our initial prototype is a wrist-worn alcohol monitor that estimates blood alcohol concentration (BAC) from transdermal sweat readings and automatically alerts a pre-selected emergency contact when levels exceed a set threshold. I designed the interactive 3D models below in three.js with Claude.

Clēar

Customer feedback showed our target market wasn't interested in tracking their BAC, so we pivoted. Clēar is a safety- and community-focused wearable that helps friends stay connected and look out for each other on nights out.

Known Limitations

  • Limited range

    Clēar uses a Bluetooth connection between bands so friends know when they are close to each other. Bluetooth only reaches a short distance, so bands can lose their connection in a big or crowded venue. A possible solution is a light or notification on the band that shows when a friend’s device leaves that range.
